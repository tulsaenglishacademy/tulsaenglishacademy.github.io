Which verbs take what?

Search common verbs and expressions followed by -ing, to + verb, an object + to + verb, or both forms.

Important: These are common sentence patterns, not absolute labels for every possible meaning. Some verbs change meaning when the next verb changes form (for example, stop smoking vs. stop to smoke). After a preposition, use a gerund: look forward to meeting (not to meet).
